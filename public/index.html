Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the early pilot program.

Does this replace my physical logbook?

No. Your physical NTSA logbook remains your legal title deed. AutoLog acts as your vehicle's permanent health and service resume—proving how well the vehicle has actually been cared for.

How does this stop clocking at Mombasa port?

By logging initial import and post-clearance check-ins, AutoLog establishes an immutable progression curve. Any attempt to record lower mileage is immediately blocked and flagged.

Can my local neighborhood mechanic use it?

Yes. Accredited garage partners can digitally stamp service records, while independent car owners can upload job cards and parts receipts for Tier 2 verification.

When does the pilot cohort launch?

Founding drivers are being invited in weekly batches across Nairobi, Kiambu, and Machakos. Early pilot members receive lifetime access to core digital passport features.
